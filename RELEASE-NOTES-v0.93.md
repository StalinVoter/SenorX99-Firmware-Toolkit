# SenorX99 v0.93

- Adds the Brazilian Portuguese translation from pt-BR.json.
- Adds Union Jack and Brazilian flag buttons above the heading and Continue button on all six pages, including pages whose actions are still locked.
- Clicking either flag switches the whole main interface immediately without restarting. The loaded ROM, modification choices, build output and step completion are preserved.
- The selected language is remembered for the next launch. Flags and the existing language selector stay synchronized.
- Flag icons are painted by Qt and do not depend on emoji fonts or additional image plugins.

Raw firmware-tool output keeps its original wording. Firmware modifications, backup and recovery behaviour are unchanged.
